Delete your Mistio data

Mistio / 미스티오 · Last updated: October 27, 2026

Mistio does not have accounts. You never sign up with an email, phone number or password, so there is no account to close. This page explains how to delete the data that Mistio holds about you.

Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Mistio and go to the My screen.
  2. Tap Delete all data and confirm.

This removes the mistake photos, answers, notes and review history stored on your device, and also asks our server to delete your anonymous usage records (plan, AI usage, ad rewards and feedback linked to your random anonymous ID).

If your device could not reach the server, the app tells you that only the on-device data was deleted. Connect to the internet and tap Delete all data again.

Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling removes the data stored on your device. Server-side anonymous records are kept only for a limited time and are then cleaned up automatically (see the Privacy Policy).

Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the app, email pkho1012@gmail.com with the subject "Mistio data deletion request". Because Mistio is anonymous, we cannot look up your records by name or email, so the in-app Delete all data is the reliable way. If you cannot use it, tell us your device model and the approximate date you used the app and we will do our best to help.

What we delete and what may remain

  • Deleted: the anonymous user record and the usage, ad-reward and feedback entries linked to it.
  • Not stored by Mistio: problem photos are not saved as files on our servers.
  • Third parties: Google AdMob handles advertising data under Google's own policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
