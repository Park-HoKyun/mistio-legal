Mistio is a study-support service that lets you review the mistakes you save and provides AI detailed explanations only when you request them. You can use it through an anonymous session without signing up.

AI detailed explanations, which use the OpenAI API, are available to users who are at least 13 years old, or at least the minimum age required in their country of residence. Users under 18 must have the permission of a parent or legal guardian. AI explanations may be inaccurate, so please check them against your textbook and the correct answer.

3. AI explanation results

AI explanations are for study reference only and do not guarantee accuracy, completeness or any particular exam result. Please verify important content with a teacher, your textbook or the official explanation.
