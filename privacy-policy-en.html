Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Mistio (the "App") does not require you to sign up and processes only the minimum information it needs.

1. Information we process

  • Mistake photos and study records: problem photos, your answers, correct answers, reasons for mistakes, notes, categories and review history are stored on your device by default.
  • AI explanation requests: only when you request a detailed explanation, the cropped and downsized problem photo, your answer, the correct answer and your reason for the mistake are sent. Photos are not saved as files on Mistio servers.
  • Anonymous authentication: the server issues a random anonymous user ID and a signed authentication token. The token is kept in your device's secure storage. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • Service usage records: we may store your plan, AI usage, ad rewards, token usage, feedback and the minimum usage times needed for troubleshooting on the server.
  • Advertising information: when you use rewarded ads, the Google AdMob SDK may process advertising identifiers, device information, ad impression and completion information, and transaction data used for server-side verification.
  • Connection information: IP addresses may be processed temporarily in memory for security and rate limiting, but the current beta storage does not separately record IP addresses.

2. How we use information

  • To provide mistake saving and review features
  • To generate the AI detailed explanations you request
  • To manage free and paid usage and ad rewards
  • To prevent abuse, respond to incidents and improve the service
  • To handle your inquiries and feedback

3. Third-party services

  • OpenAI API: when you request a detailed explanation, the problem photo you cropped, the answers you entered and your reason for the mistake are sent for the purpose of generating the explanation.
  • Google AdMob: may process the information needed to show rewarded ads and confirm ad completion.
  • The App does not sell your information.

4. Retention

  • Mistake photos and study records: kept on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App.
  • AI cost and usage events on the server: kept for up to 180 days by default, then cleaned up.
  • Anonymous user records that have been inactive for a long time: may be cleaned up after 365 days by default.
  • Minimal token identifiers used to prevent reuse of deleted sessions: may be kept for up to 730 days by default.
  • Where the law requires retention, we may keep information for the required period.

5. Backups and moving devices

  • Android automatic cloud backup is disabled.
  • A manual backup from the App may include problem photos, answers and notes.
  • Manual backups are currently not encrypted, so you should keep them somewhere only you can access.

6. Age requirements

AI detailed explanations are available to users who are at least 13 years old, or at least the minimum age required in their country of residence. Users under 18 must have the permission of a parent or legal guardian. We do not collect your date of birth; we only store on your device whether you confirmed that you meet the requirements.

7. Your rights and deletion

  • You can delete individual mistake records.
  • Under "Delete all data" you can request deletion of the data on your device and of your anonymous usage records on the server.
  • If the connection to the server fails, only the data on your device may be deleted first; the App will tell you separately whether the server records were deleted.
  • For privacy-related questions, contact pkho1012@gmail.com.

8. Using photos safely

When you crop a problem photo, please make sure it does not include unnecessary personal information such as names, school names, student ID numbers, faces or contact details. Do not send photos containing other people's personal information without their consent.

9. Changes to this policy

If the service or the law changes, we will update this policy and notify you in the App or on a web page.
